A.1: Shoot the Pictures

I shot three sets of photos, one for each of the three mosaics in A.4. For each set I kept the center of projection fixed and only rotated the camera between shots, so the images in a set are related by a projective transformation (a homography). I took each set's photos back to back so nothing in the scene moved and the lighting stayed the same. The fields of view overlap a lot, which gives plenty of shared points to click as correspondences when I recover the homographies in A.2.
